The analytical problem

Historical events need a defensible timeline.

I reconstructed subscription cohorts from transactions, combined them with listening behaviour and built 38 predictors using events strictly before each scoring date. A seven-day intervention lead and a 30-day renewal-gap outcome keep the targeting question explicit.

The workflow audits 392 million original listening records, tests duplicate and time-boundary handling, and separates future outcomes from past features. Backdated records in a refreshed release are tested as an alternative label source.

Evaluation sequence

  1. Train on May–August 2016 snapshots.
  2. Select the model on October data.
  3. Calibrate probabilities on December data.
  4. Freeze the specification before February evaluation.
  5. Compare source definitions and commercial assumptions.

Event dates are observable; historical ingestion availability is unproven.

Predictive evidence

Strong concentration of risk, with clear limits.

The selected logistic model combines transaction and listening features. At 10% capacity, 68,040 contacts contain 25,155 of the 38,037 reconstructed churners.

Commercial judgement

A source update can reverse the funding decision.

On the common population, a fixed list of 67,930 contacts is assessed under both label sources. With assumed retained contribution of 60 generic currency units (CU) and contact/offer cost of 3.50 CU per customer, the required conditional save rate rises from 16.27% to 24.94%.

Assuming 20% of would-be churners are saved gives +54,445 CU under baseline labels and −47,087 CU under refreshed labels. These are scenario estimates, not realised profit or measured treatment effects.

Commercial assumptions, capacity comparisons and sensitivity.

What would justify action?

  • Confirm source version and operational data availability.
  • Replace illustrative values with measured unit economics.
  • Validate coverage for customers with missing records.
  • Run a customer-randomised offer experiment with renewal and contribution outcomes.

No campaign has been run and no incremental retention has been measured.

Controls and limitations

Make the uncertainty part of the recommendation.

Independent checks reconcile 7,000 sampled labels, 16,100 listening-feature values, 96 model metrics and 5,604 commercial values. Versioned SQL, pinned dependencies, a frozen evaluation plan and a separate final reproduction record make the work inspectable.

Final reproduction and publication record · Proposed experiment

Limits that remain

  • One historical subscription dataset; no production deployment.
  • 77.2% of final-test customers also appear in training snapshots.
  • Weaker ranking for customers missing member or listening records.
  • 22 unresolved differences from supplied competition labels.
  • Value prioritisation is a proxy sensitivity, not validated lifetime value.
  • Churn risk is not an estimate of treatment responsiveness.
